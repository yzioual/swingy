# Swingy - Java Arc

In this project, I created a Java RPG about a poor fisher trying to get home alive. This is my 2nd project in the Java arc.

## Story

You are a poor fisher. Every day you dive off your small boat to catch a fish or two. If you don't, you and your family will starve.
Today's dive begins in the middle of the map, where you spear a silver fish. Now you have to get back to shore alive with it. But the blood and the fish attract trouble, and your worst fears become real.

## How to Play

- **Goal:** Carry your fish to the border of the map. The border is the shore. If you reach it, your family survives one more day.
- **The start:** You begin in the middle of the map and spear the fish.
- **The danger:** On your way to shore, you run into your fears. Each time one appears, you have **two options**:
  1. **Kick back:** Swim back the way you came, where the current can hold you.
  2. **Fight back:** Use your salvaged dive gear: a spear, a wetsuit, or a mask.

**If you fail to overcome your fears, you will never make it back.**
